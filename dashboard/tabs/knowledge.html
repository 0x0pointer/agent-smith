Connect a notes vault

A Source is a small search service that runs on this computer over a folder of Markdown notes. Nothing leaves the machine. Once one is connected, Smith can look things up in it during a scan and save what it learns.

  1. Get Source from github.com/0x0pointer/Source and install it.
  2. Start it on your notes folder: 
    python serve_http.py --vault ./source --port 7801
  3. Register it in sources.yaml at the top of the agent-smith folder: 
    sources:
  team-notes: {url: "http://127.0.0.1:7801"}
  4. Reload this page. The vault appears here, and in the Details step when you start a scan.
